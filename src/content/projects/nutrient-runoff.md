Runoff from coastal agriculture changes the water a keystone species lives in. This study
measures what that does to the organism's oxygen production and photosynthetic rate, and
to the carbon concentrating mechanisms it uses to pull inorganic carbon out of seawater.

A keystone species is a load-bearing wall: the consequences of a change in its productivity
are not confined to it.

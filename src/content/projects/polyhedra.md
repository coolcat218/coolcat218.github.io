The premise arose from a curiosity about repeating shapes and objects — figures that
contain smaller copies of themselves, like Sierpiński's triangle and carpet.

Slicing an n-sided polyhedron n ways and repeating the operation raises the obvious
question: does the sequence converge, and to what?
